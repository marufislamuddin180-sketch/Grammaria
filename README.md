# GramMaru: The Quest for Perfect Syntax

A retro fantasy educational RPG built with React 19, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS. Players battle language-distorting monsters across 5 perilous dungeons to restore grammatical order to the realm of GramMaru.

## ⚔️ Game Overview

- **The World**: The "Syntax Error" curse has mutated corrupted sentences into vicious monsters.
- **Hero Classes**:
  - **Syntax Knight**: Resilient guardian with high HP (120 HP) wielding the *Blade of Proper Agreement*.
  - **Vocab Mage**: High spell damage specialist (40 Base DMG) casting *Lexical Surge*.
  - **Punctuation Rogue**: Agile combatant with extra potions (3x Elixir) throwing the *Semicolon Dagger*.
- **5 Monster Sectors & Curriculum**:
  1. *Present Tense Goblin* (Sector 1) - Master of the 4 Present Tenses (Simple, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous).
  2. *Past Tense Troll* (Sector 2) - Warden of the 4 Past Tenses (Simple, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous).
  3. *Future Chrono Dragon* (Sector 3) - Scourge of the 4 Future Tenses (Simple, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous).
  4. *Past-Future Chimera* (Sector 4) - Abomination of the 4 Past Future Tenses (Simple, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous).
  5. *The Great Conjunction Overlord* (Sector 5 Boss) - Master of all Conjunctions (Coordinating FANBOYS, Subordinating, Correlative, Conjunctive Adverbs).
- **Combat & Education Engine**:
  - Turn-based battles where choosing the correct grammatical sentence slashes the enemy.
  - Immediate educational breakdown explaining the grammar rule behind each choice.
  - Item management with *Elixir of Eloquence* potions.
  - Level progression, EXP scaling, health restoration, and potion loot drops.
  - Interactive Grimoire & Bestiary reference book for all grammar rules and monsters.
  - Synthesized 8-bit retro sound effects via Web Audio API.
